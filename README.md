# Water Utility Non-Revenue Water Recovery

Quantify apparent and real water losses, prioritize recoverable billing leakage, and measure repair economics.

**Primary buyer:** Municipal and investor-owned water utilities. **Evidence:** production meters, district meters, customer meters, billing, pressure, leak events, authorized unbilled use, meter accuracy, repairs, and collections.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Water balance registry
- Production meter ingestion
- District meter ingestion
- Customer consumption ingestion
- Authorized unbilled use
- Meter under-registration
- Data handling error detection
- Unauthorized consumption
- Real loss calculation
- Leak event prioritization
- Pressure management analysis
- Meter replacement economics
- Billing correction workflow
- Recovered revenue tracking
- District loss analytics
